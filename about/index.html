About

Who we are and how we work.

Global Art Monitor follows auction activity and the structure of the art market, and tries to be exact about what a published price does and does not show.

Who publishes this

Global Art Monitor is published by Artiscope, the group behind Grove Gallery in London. Grove Gallery sells work by artists covered on this site, including Zara Muse and Konn Artiss. Please read our coverage with that interest in mind.

We think the best answer to that interest is to show our working. Every figure carries a source. Lower results and unconfirmed lots are listed next to the highest ones. Our features say plainly what a small auction record cannot prove.

Method

We record public auction sales only. For each lot we note the sale date, the house, the sale title, the lot number, the estimate, the hammer price, the price with buyer's premium and the currency.

  • Hammer and premium stay separate. Where a house publishes only the total, we work the hammer price back using that house's buyer's premium and label it "our calculation".
  • Currency stays as sold. We do not convert prices in the tables. If a conversion appears in an article, it carries the date and rate.
  • Gaps are shown. If we cannot confirm a figure it reads "to confirm". We do not estimate missing prices.
  • Unsold lots count. When we can document a lot that was offered and not sold, it goes in the record.

Sources

Our first source is always the auction house's own lot page. Where a house does not publish a figure, we use public auction databases such as Invaluable, MutualArt and Artnet, and say which. News reports are used for context and are linked.

The source for each result is linked in the last column of every table.

Corrections

If a figure here is wrong or out of date, we want to know. Email info@grovegallery.com with the page, the figure and your source. We correct the record and note material changes on the page concerned.

The monthly market note

A short email with new documented results and new research, sent when there is something to report. To join the list, email info@grovegallery.com with the subject line "Subscribe".

Contact

For questions, press enquiries or anything else, use the contact page.

Not advice

Nothing on this site is investment, tax or legal advice. Auction results describe past sales of particular works. They do not predict what any work will sell for in future.
