Ana Segovia

Ana Segovia was born in Mexico City in 1991 and works there. Segovia paints in oil, mostly figures taken from Mexican cowboy imagery and golden-age cinema, and has recently added video and charcoal drawing.

About the artist

We have found no public auction result for Ana Segovia as of 25 November 2026. That does not mean no work has ever been sold at auction, only that none is documented in the sources we checked.

The record so far is a gallery one. kurimanzutto announced on 15 November 2023 that Segovia had joined the gallery, and gave the artist a solo booth at Frieze London in October 2025. We found no published gallery prices. Gallery prices and auction prices are different things and are not compared here.

Life and career

Segovia's paintings start from film stills of the golden age of Mexican cinema, from the mid-1930s to the late 1950s, and from classic Westerns, according to Galerie. In 2024 the Venice Biennale showed Pos' se acabó este cantar (2021), Segovia's first film, with two charros in suits made by the grandson of Jorge Negrete's tailor.

In 2025 kurimanzutto gave Segovia a solo booth at Frieze London with the Ramón series, built around an invented Mexican cowboy, according to AnOther.

Why the work is valued

MOCA Los Angeles gave Segovia a solo exhibition, MOCA Focus, from 23 November 2024 to 4 May 2025, and bought Through Mario's perspective (2024). Segovia showed in the 60th Venice Biennale in 2024. kurimanzutto lists works in the Louisiana Museum of Modern Art, the Denver Art Museum and the Pérez Art Museum Miami.
