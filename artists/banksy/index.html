Banksy

Banksy is an anonymous artist who began as a teenage graffiti writer in Bristol in the 1990s. He works mainly in spray paint through hand-cut stencils, on walls and on canvas, and also makes screenprint editions and sculptural pieces from found objects.

About the artist

The table below lists the five most recent public sales of unique works that we could document, all from autumn 2026. The prices for Angel and the 2001 Laugh Now are stated with buyer's premium on the Bonhams lot pages. Phillips' lot page for Keep Ou T gives "Sold For £1,612,500" without naming the basis; we show it as the price with premium.

Two figures are still to confirm. Heritage Auctions confirms that Rat & Heart sold, and Sotheby's lists the 2002 Laugh Now as closed, but both show the price only to signed-in users. Banksy Explained, a tracking site, reports USD 387,500 for Rat & Heart and GBP 256,000 for Laugh Now. We have not been able to check those figures against the auction houses.

Unsold lots are part of the record. Sotheby's withdrew a signed Flower Thrower Triptych (2019, VIP edition 7/15) from its November 2026 New York sale. Grannies (2006), offered at Sotheby's London on 15 October 2026 with an estimate of GBP 1.5 million to 2 million, and Rat Clock with Headset (2019), offered at Sotheby's New York on 7 October 2026, are reported as unsold. Sotheby's pages show no result for either.

Prints and other editions are tracked separately, because an edition of hundreds is not comparable with a unique canvas. They will be added once we have checked each result with the auction house.
