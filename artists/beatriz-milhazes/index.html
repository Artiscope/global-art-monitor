Beatriz Milhazes

Beatriz Milhazes is a Brazilian painter, born in Rio de Janeiro in 1960, where she still works. She paints motifs on plastic sheets and transfers them to canvas, building dense layers of flowers, circles and stripes.

About the artist

The table lists the two unique works we could document from 2026. Phillips' lot page for Paraty gives "Sold For US$516,000" without naming the basis; we show it as the price with premium. HENI News reports these prices without saying whether they include the buyer's premium. We show them in the premium column because, where we could compare, HENI's figures match the totals with premium reported elsewhere. Treat them as reported, not confirmed by the auction house. HENI reports that Moinho vermelho (Red Mill) sold below its low estimate.

Unsold lots are part of the record. Ovale (2002), a collage on paper offered by Phillips in New York with an estimate of US$30,000 to 50,000, shows no sold price on its lot page.

Editions are tracked separately. Bolha de Sabão (Soap Bubble), a 2024 collage edition of 40, sold for GBP 9,675 in a Phillips online editions sale in London in June 2026. It is not comparable with a unique canvas.

Why the work is valued

Milhazes represented Brazil at the Venice Biennale in 2003. Her paintings are in the collections of the Museum of Modern Art, the Guggenheim and the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York. In São Paulo, MASP and Itaú Cultural held a joint survey of more than 170 works, Avenida Paulista, from December 2020 to mid 2021. In 2025 the Guggenheim gave her the first New York museum show devoted to her work, Rigor and Beauty.

On this site her highest documented results are for large unique canvases. Her collage editions sell at a very different level and are tracked separately.
