Beatriz Milhazes

Beatriz Milhazes is a Brazilian painter, born in Rio de Janeiro in 1960, where she still works. She paints motifs on plastic sheets and transfers them to canvas, building dense layers of flowers, circles and stripes.

About the artist

The table lists the two unique works we could document from 2026. Phillips' lot page for Paraty gives "Sold For US$516,000" without naming the basis; we show it as the price with premium. Christie's lot page for Moinho vermelho (Red Mill), lot 5 in London on 25 June 2026, gives a price realised of GBP 584,200 against an estimate of GBP 600,000 to 800,000, so it sold below its low estimate.

Unsold lots are part of the record. Ovale (2002), a collage on paper offered by Phillips in New York with an estimate of US$30,000 to 50,000, shows no sold price on its lot page.

Editions are tracked separately. Bolha de Sabão (Soap Bubble), a 2024 collage edition of 40, sold for GBP 9,675 in a Phillips online editions sale in London in June 2026. It is not comparable with a unique canvas.

Life and career

Milhazes studied journalism before training at the Parque Lage School of Visual Arts in Rio, and took part in the 1984 exhibition Como Vai Você, Geração 80?, according to Studio International. She grew up under Brazil's military dictatorship of 1964 to 1985.

She developed her transfer technique in 1989 and began making collages in 2003, using sweet and chocolate wrappers, according to Ocula. Her first public commission was the facade of Selfridges in Manchester in 2004. In 2023 Turner Contemporary in Margate gave her Maresias, her first institutional show in the UK in more than 20 years.

Why the work is valued

Milhazes represented Brazil at the Venice Biennale in 2003. Her paintings are in the collections of the Museum of Modern Art, the Guggenheim and the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York. In São Paulo, MASP and Itaú Cultural held a joint survey of more than 170 works, Avenida Paulista, from December 2020 to mid 2021. In 2025 the Guggenheim gave her the first New York museum show devoted to her work, Rigor and Beauty.

On this site her highest documented results are for large unique canvases. Her collage editions sell at a very different level and are tracked separately.

Key works, and what reaches auction

O Diamante (2002)

Acrylic on canvas, 250 x 381 cm

One of her widest canvases, in the collection of the 'la Caixa' Foundation. Very large canvases from the early 2000s are the group that carries her highest prices.

O Turista (2004 to 2005)

Acrylic on canvas

199 x 195 cm, in the Cranford Collection. Our reading: a canvas so packed with circles, stripes and flowers that the eye never settles in one place.

Collages (from 2003)

Paper, wrappers and printed material

Unique works on paper, separate from her print editions. They sell at a different level from the canvases and should be compared with each other.
