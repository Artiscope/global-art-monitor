Artist Market Profiles

Dana Awartani

Dana Awartani was born in Jeddah in 1987 and lives between Jeddah and New York. Trained in traditional Islamic arts, she works with geometry, embroidery, natural dyes and tile, often to address the loss of heritage.

About the artist

No auction result for a unique work by Dana Awartani has been confirmed on an auction house page, so the table is empty. Three lots were found.

At Sotheby's, Dodecahedron Within an Icosahedron (2016, wood, copper and glass, number 3 from an edition of 3 plus an artist's proof) was offered as lot 1 in October 2019 against an estimate of £18,000 to 22,000. Sotheby's shows the result only to signed-in readers, so it is to confirm. As an edition, it would not go in our table of unique works.

At Christie's New York, Abjad Hawaz Series (2016, shell gold, ink and gouache on paper, in 28 parts) was offered on 14 December 2022 (lot 244) and again on 18 December 2024 (lot 228). The listings database Very Important Lot reports the 2024 offer as sold for US$6,930 and gives no result for 2022. We have not been able to check either on Christie's own pages, so both stay to confirm.

Life and career

Awartani trained at Central Saint Martins and the Prince's School of Traditional Arts in London, according to Berlin Art Link. She works with craftspeople, including natural dyers in Trivandrum in South India and Syrian refugees in Jordan, and has said she makes no duplicates.

Why the work is valued

Awartani represented Saudi Arabia at the 61st Venice Biennale, which ran from 9 May to 22 November 2026, in a pavilion curated by Antonia Carver. She also showed in the 60th Biennale in 2024. Ocula lists works in the Guggenheim, the British Museum and the Louvre Abu Dhabi.
