Artist Market Profiles

Eko Nugroho

Eko Nugroho is an Indonesian artist, born in 1977, who lives and works in Yogyakarta. He makes murals, paintings, embroidery, batik and sculpture, with masked comic-book figures, often working with local communities.

Global Art Monitor has documented 3 auction lots by Eko Nugroho, offered between 10 November 2022 and 2 May 2025 at Phillips and Christie's. The most recent on our record is Topi untuk Haring (A Hat for Haring) (2012), Phillips, 2 May 2025, sold for HK$25,400 with buyer's premium. Source: Phillips lot page.

About the artist

Only three sales of unique works could be documented, all checked on the auction houses' own pages. Christie's shows a "Price realised", which under its usual terms includes the buyer's premium; Phillips' "Sold For" figure is shown as the price with premium. Topi untuk Haring sold well below its low estimate.

Unsold and unpriced lots are part of the record. Garden Full of Blooming Democracy #9 (2017), offered by Phillips in Hong Kong on 29 March 2025 with an estimate of HKD 120,000 to 180,000, shows no sold price. Seneng Kuwi Larang Regane (2009), offered at Sotheby's Hong Kong in November 2021, shows its result only to signed-in users. Indonesian auction houses were not checked.

Life and career

Nugroho studied at the Indonesian Institute of the Arts in Yogyakarta and founded the comic magazine Daging Tumbuh in 2000. His first solo show was at Cemeti Art House in 2002, according to The Jakarta Post.

From 2006 his work reached international museums: the Asia Pacific Triennial in Brisbane, a solo show at the Musée d'Art Moderne de Paris in 2012 and the Indonesian Pavilion at the Venice Biennale in 2013. In 2013 he also designed a Louis Vuitton scarf, made in an edition of 500, The Jakarta Post reports.

Why the work is valued

Eko Nugroho was one of five artists chosen for Indonesia's pavilion at the 55th Venice Biennale in 2013, where he showed a bamboo raft work, Menghasut Badai-badai (Instigating Storms).

His auction record outside Indonesia is thin. We have not yet checked Indonesian auction houses, so the picture here is incomplete.

Key works, and what reaches auction

Embroideries (from the 2000s)

Machine embroidery on fabric

Stitched by tailors from his designs; he has said finding people who could do it was hard at first. Illusion (2007) is in the Singapore Art Museum, and Trap Costum for President 1 (2011) is in our table.

Paintings (from 2000s)

Acrylic on canvas

Creamy policy (2010) is a typical example, 200 x 150 cm. Paintings and embroideries sell at different levels and should be compared within their own group.

Reading the prices

Our record has three results, at Christie's in London and Phillips in Hong Kong, in two currencies. That is too few to describe a level, so treat each price as a single result.
