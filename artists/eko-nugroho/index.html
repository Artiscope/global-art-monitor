Artist Market Profiles

Eko Nugroho

Eko Nugroho is an Indonesian artist, born in 1977, who lives and works in Yogyakarta. He makes murals, paintings, embroidery, batik and sculpture, with masked comic-book figures, often working with local communities.

About the artist

Only three sales of unique works could be documented, all checked on the auction houses' own pages. Christie's shows a "Price realised", which under its usual terms includes the buyer's premium; Phillips' "Sold For" figure is shown as the price with premium. Topi untuk Haring sold well below its low estimate.

Unsold and unpriced lots are part of the record. Garden Full of Blooming Democracy #9 (2017), offered by Phillips in Hong Kong on 29 March 2025 with an estimate of HKD 120,000 to 180,000, shows no sold price. Seneng Kuwi Larang Regane (2009), offered at Sotheby's Hong Kong in November 2021, shows its result only to signed-in users. Indonesian auction houses were not checked.

Why the work is valued

Eko Nugroho was one of five artists chosen for Indonesia's pavilion at the 55th Venice Biennale in 2013, where he showed a bamboo raft work, Menghasut Badai-badai (Instigating Storms).

His auction record outside Indonesia is thin. We have not yet checked Indonesian auction houses, so the picture here is incomplete.
