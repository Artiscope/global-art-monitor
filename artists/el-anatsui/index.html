El Anatsui

El Anatsui is a Ghanaian sculptor, born in Anyako in 1944, who has taught and worked in Nsukka, Nigeria, since 1975. He is best known for large wall hangings made from flattened aluminium bottle caps joined with copper wire. He received the Golden Lion for Lifetime Achievement at the Venice Biennale in 2015.

About the artist

Only two public auction lots from 2025 and 2026 could be documented, both at Sotheby's in London. G6 (2023) was offered on 4 March 2026 in a group of works donated by artists to support the Royal Academy, against an estimate of GBP 800,000 to 1.2 million. Artdaily reports GBP 742,400 (US$995,000), without naming the basis. Sotheby's shows the result only to signed-in users.

Hesitant Rivers (2012) closed on 16 October 2025 with the same estimate. Sotheby's shows the result only to signed-in users and we found no published report of it, so its price is to confirm.

Two results are too few to describe his market. Artnet lists further lots offered in 2026, but shows their prices only to subscribers. The widely reported US$2.2 million sale of LuwVor I at Frieze New York in May 2026 was a gallery sale, not an auction, and is not in the table.

These figures are a record of past public sales. They are not a valuation and they are not advice. For how to read them, start with what an auction result actually tells collectors.
