Artist Market Profiles

Frank Canvas

Frank Canvas is a London-based artist working under an alias. A press release issued in December 2022 by Nomad Arts Group announced his first series, Socially Awkward Elephant, and a gallery in the Artiscope group sells his signed limited edition prints with one-line titles such as Choose Your Reality and Shopaholic.

About the artist

We have found no public auction result for Frank Canvas as of 25 November 2026. We searched the lot archives of Phillips and Tate Ward and general auction listings. That does not mean no work has ever been sold at auction, only that none is documented in the sources we checked.

The gallery offers Choose Your Reality as a signed edition of 125. Those are gallery prices, not auction results. The gallery is part of the Artiscope group, which publishes this site.

Why the work is valued

We found no museum show, collection or independent review in the sources we read. Almost all published information comes from the gallery that sells the work or from a press release. The gallery records his work at South Place Hotel in London from September 2024.
