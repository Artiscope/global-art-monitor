Artist Market Profiles

Gerhard Richter

Gerhard Richter is a German painter, born in Dresden in 1932, who moved to West Germany in 1961 and lives in Cologne. He works in two main strands: blurred paintings made from photographs, and abstract paintings dragged across the canvas with a large squeegee.

About the artist

The table lists the five most recent public sales of unique paintings we could document. Christie's Hong Kong sold Abstraktes Bild (1991) on 27 March 2026 for HKD 92,100,000 with premium, a hammer price of HKD 76,000,000, as reported by TheValue.

Two London results from October 2026 are added. Phillips sold Mathis (1983), from the collection of Marcel Brient, on 15 October for £5,839,000, just under its £6 million low estimate, according to its own lot page. The same evening Sotheby's sold Abstraktes Bild (1992) for about £6.7 million with fees and a hammer price of about £5.4 million, as reported by Artcentron. Those figures are rounded, so they stay to confirm.

Several figures come from press reports that round them, so the table shows them as to confirm. Christie's gives US$35,135,000 with premium for Kerze (Candle) (1982). Working back from Christie's rates at the time gives a hammer price of exactly US$30,000,000, which matches The Art Newspaper's report of about US$30 million, so we show it as our calculation. The same paper reports Abstraktes Bild (1991), sold at Christie's New York on 19 November 2026, at about US$7.6 million hammer and US$9.4 million with fees. Phillips reports Abstraktes Bild (522-1) at over HKD 23 million (US$2.9 million), without naming the basis. HENI News reports these prices without saying whether they include the buyer's premium. We show them in the premium column because, where we could compare, HENI's figures match the totals with premium reported elsewhere. Treat them as reported, not confirmed by the auction house.

Unsold lots are part of the record. At Christie's New York on 19 November 2026, bidding for Abstraktes Bild (1990) stopped at US$5.7 million against an estimate of US$6 million to 8 million, and it did not sell.

Photographs and overpainted photographs are tracked separately. Christie's sold Strip (2012) for US$1,765,300 with premium in May 2026.

Why the work is valued

Richter received the Praemium Imperiale for painting and the Golden Lion at the Venice Biennale, both in 1997. Tate Modern's retrospective, Gerhard Richter: Panorama, ran from 6 October 2011 to 8 January 2012, and Tate described him then as widely regarded as one of the most important artists working today. His stained glass window for Cologne Cathedral was unveiled in 2007.

In 2026 alone we list sales of his paintings in London, New York and Hong Kong. Prices depend on which strand of his work a piece belongs to, and on its size and date, so abstract paintings, photo paintings, photographs and editions should each be compared with their own kind. That is why photographs and overpainted photographs are kept out of the main table.

These figures are a record of past public sales. They are not a valuation and they are not advice. For how to read them, start with what an auction result actually tells collectors.
