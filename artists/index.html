How we report a price

Two prices, never one

Hammer and premium-inclusive prices are shown in separate columns and never merged.

Currency as sold

Figures stay in the currency of the sale. Any conversion carries its date and rate.

A source on every row

Each result links to the lot page or database it came from. Gaps are labelled, not filled.
