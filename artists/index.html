How we report a price

Two prices, never one

Hammer and premium-inclusive prices are shown in separate columns and never merged.

Currency as sold

Figures stay in the currency of the sale. Any conversion carries its date and rate.

A source on every row

Each result links to the lot page or database it came from. Gaps are labelled, not filled.

MethodSourcesCorrectionsContact

Global Art Monitor does not sell art. It is part of Artiscope and reports public auctions and their results for the artists it covers. A gallery elsewhere in the Artiscope group sells work by some of those artists, including Zara Muse, Konn Artiss, Zen Xander, Los-Talgia, Frank Canvas, Robi Walters and Amanza Smith. Nothing on this site is investment advice. Past auction results do not predict future prices. More about who we are
