About the artist

In June 2025 the name was attached to a protest piece in Venice timed to the wedding of Jeff Bezos. In October 2025, during Frieze week in London, Konn Artiss left large blocks of ice containing effigies of well known artists outside the fair and outside galleries and auction houses.

Auction activity is recent and small. We have documented two results so far, both at Tate Ward and both for works dated 2025.

Why the work is valued

Konn Artiss is known for the interventions described above, which drew coverage from The Art Newspaper and the art press, rather than for museum shows. We have not found work in a public collection or a museum exhibition in the sources we have read. With two auction results, both at one house, there is not yet a market record to describe.

Global Art Monitor does not sell art. A gallery elsewhere in the Artiscope group sells work by Konn Artiss.
