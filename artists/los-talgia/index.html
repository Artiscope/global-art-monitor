Los-Talgia

Los-Talgia is an anonymous collective. The gallery that sells its work describes it as a small group of artists from different generations who work under one name, with no names and no hierarchy. The works are hand-finished objects built around the look of old games consoles, video sleeves, cassette labels and packaging.

About the artist

We found one public auction result. Handheld Icon Collection (2026), from an edition of 55 unique works, sold as lot 109 in Tate Ward's online sale on Artsy that closed on 13 August 2026, for £1,062 with premium, against an estimate of £800 to 1,200. Tate Ward's lot page describes it as 3D-printed polymer with acrylic and spray paint, resin and mixed media, 28 x 23 cm in the artist's frame, with a numbered collector card in an acrylic case.

The gallery that sells the work lists Handheld Icon works at £495 and £695. Those are gallery asking prices, set before sale, and an auction result is a different kind of figure. One result is not enough to compare the two.

Why the work is valued

We found no museum show, collection or independent review of Los-Talgia in the sources we read. Almost all published information comes from the gallery that sells the work. That gallery is part of the Artiscope group, which publishes this site.

Reading the prices

The edition is described as 55 unique works: each piece is finished by hand, so no two are identical, but all share one design. That sits between a print edition and a one-off painting, and should be compared with neither.
