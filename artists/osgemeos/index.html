OSGEMEOS

OSGEMEOS are the Brazilian twin brothers Gustavo and Otavio Pandolfo, born in 1974, who started as graffiti artists in São Paulo. Their murals, paintings and installations show thin, yellow-skinned figures in patterned clothes, often with sequins, sound or found doors.

About the artist

The table lists the five most recent public sales of unique works we could document, each checked against the auction house's own lot page. Christie's shows a "Price realised", which under its usual terms includes the buyer's premium; Bonhams states "inc. premium". Phillips' lot pages give "Sold For" without naming the basis; we show it as the price with premium.

Unsold and unpriced lots are part of the record. I See The Light (2024) was sold by Phillips in London in October 2025 to benefit a charity, with no price published. All Connected (2020), offered at Sotheby's New York on 12 November 2024, shows its result only to signed-in users. An Untitled canvas of 2011, offered by Phillips in London on 30 June 2023 with an estimate of GBP 150,000 to 200,000, shows no sold price.

Why the work is valued

In 2008 OSGEMEOS were among six artists invited to paint the river front of Tate Modern for its Street Art exhibition, from 23 May to 25 August, which Tate called the first major public museum display of street art in London. Tate's release noted that they started painting graffiti in 1987.

Their murals stay on the walls they are painted on. What reaches auction is studio work: paintings, sculpture and installations, which is a much smaller pool.
