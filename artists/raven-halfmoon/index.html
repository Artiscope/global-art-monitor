Raven Halfmoon

Raven Halfmoon was born in Norman, Oklahoma, in 1991 and still works there. She is a citizen of the Caddo Nation and builds large figures in stoneware by coiling clay, some of them over 12 feet tall.

About the artist

Two public auction results are documented, both at Phillips in New York in 2024 and both for stoneware sculptures made in 2020. On 15 May 2024, OKLA HOMMA OHOYO (lot 310) sold for US$22,860 with buyer's premium against an estimate of US$10,000 to 15,000. On 25 September 2024, Two Sides To Every Story and Historic Glory (lot 55) sold for US$20,320 with buyer's premium against the same estimate. Phillips does not print hammer prices on its lot pages, so those stay to confirm.

Two results are too few to describe a trend. Her largest works are documented in museums rather than at auction: Crystal Bridges bought Caddo Woman Warrior (2021) in 2024.

Life and career

Halfmoon holds a bachelor's degree in anthropology and studio art from the University of Arkansas, and is a citizen of the Caddo Nation who also has Choctaw, Delaware and Otoe heritage, according to The Creative Independent. In 2024 she added bronze and stone to her stoneware, and showed NEESH + SOKU (MOON + SUN) at Salon 94, her first show with the gallery.

Why the work is valued

Her first travelling solo exhibition, Flags of Our Mothers, opened at the Aldrich Contemporary Art Museum in 2023 and went on to the Bemis Center (2024), The Contemporary Austin (2025), the Ogden Museum of Southern Art (2025 to 2026) and Ballroom Marfa (2026). She was a 2023 Eiteljorg Contemporary Art Fellow and a 2024 LOEWE Craft Prize finalist, and was in the 2026 Whitney Biennial. Crystal Bridges, the Detroit Institute of Arts and ICA Miami hold her work.

Key works, and what reaches auction

Flagbearer (2022)

Stoneware and glaze, about 12 feet

Her largest figures are made for museum shows such as Flags of Our Mothers. Firing alone takes two to three weeks, she told Southwest Contemporary.

The Guardians (2024)

Bronze, nine feet

One of her first bronzes. Bronze can be cast in editions, so check the edition when one appears at auction.

In the artist's words

Reading the prices

Her two documented results are both for stoneware figures of 2020 at Phillips. Stoneware, bronze and travertine are different materials with different costs and editions, so they should not be compared with one another.
