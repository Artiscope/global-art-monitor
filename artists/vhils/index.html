Vhils

Vhils is the Portuguese artist Alexandre Farto, born in Lisbon in 1987. He started in Lisbon graffiti and makes portraits by cutting, drilling, etching or blasting into walls, posters, doors and metal.

About the artist

Only one unique work could be documented with a price: Attrition series #01, sold at Bonhams in London in October 2025 for GBP 10,240 including premium. Sotheby's sold carved works in 2025, among them Depict Series #08 and Logo and Pattern Series #03 in July, but shows their results only to signed-in users.

Editions are tracked separately. Four signed prints sold in Artcurial's Urban Online sale in Paris, which closed on 3 April 2026: Tenuous (screenprint, 73/250) EUR 1,986, Glimpse (lithograph, 81/140) EUR 1,258, Debris (screenprint, 165/300) EUR 861 and an untitled lithograph (13/25) EUR 596. Artcurial's pages show these as "Sold" without naming the basis.

Life and career

Farto started as an illegal graffiti writer at 13 and by 16 or 17 was questioning it, he told designboom in 2017. He studied in London at about 19 and painted at the Cans Festival there in 2008, alongside Banksy, according to Euronews.

His studio, founded in 2010, works from Barreiro on the outskirts of Lisbon with a team of about 15, and he developed his explosives method around 2012, according to Hypebeast.

Why the work is valued

Vhils has had institutional shows in his home city. MAAT in Lisbon showed Prisma, a video installation filmed in nine cities, from 30 March to 5 September 2022. In early 2026 MUDE, Lisbon's design museum, showed Selected Editions 2008-2024, a look back at his printed editions.

His carved walls stay where they are made, so his auction record is built from studio pieces and prints. Unique carved works and prints sell at very different levels.

Reading the prices

Read the medium line first. Carved posters, carved wood or metal, explosive works and prints are all sold under his name, and they are priced very differently.
