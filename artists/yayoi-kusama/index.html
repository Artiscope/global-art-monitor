Artist Market Profiles

Yayoi Kusama

Yayoi Kusama was a Japanese artist, born in Matsumoto in 1929, who died in Tokyo on 14 August 2026. She worked in New York from 1958 to 1973. She is best known for her Infinity Nets, canvases covered edge to edge in small looped brushmarks, and for her polka dots and mirror rooms.

About the artist

The table below lists the five most recent public sales of unique works that we could document, all from 2026. On 16 November 2026 Sotheby's sold No. 3P.B., an Infinity Net of 1960, in its Now and Contemporary Evening Auction in New York against an estimate of US$10 million to US$15 million. The Art Newspaper reports a hammer price of about US$12.5 million and about US$15 million with premium. HENI News gives US$15.05 million and Artsy US$15.1 million, with fees. All three are rounded, so the table shows both figures as to confirm. The sources agree that it is a new auction record for the artist, but disagree on the record it replaced.

HENI News reports these prices without saying whether they include the buyer's premium. We show them in the premium column because, where we could compare, HENI's figures match the totals with premium reported elsewhere. Treat them as reported, not confirmed by the auction house. HENI rounds the prices for INFINITY-NETS (OOAXT) (about US$1.59 million) and Infinity Nets of 1970 (about KRW 2 billion, US$1.32 million), so those stay to confirm.

Two other London sales from March 2026 fall just outside the five: Pumpkin (1991) at GBP 381,000 and Infinity Nets (1960) at about GBP 2.12 million, both at Christie's and both reported by HENI News.
