Zara Muse

Zara Muse is the working name of Alexandra Johnson, a London painter born in 1971. She paints in acrylic with a palette knife and spatula, mostly large portraits of women built up in thick layers of colour.

About the artist

Her work first appeared at auction in September 2024, when In Her Own Light sold at Tate Ward in London. Since then her paintings have been offered at Tate Ward, Bonhams and Christie's.
