Artist Market Profiles

Zara Muse

Zara Muse is the working name of Alexandra Johnson, a London painter born in 1971. She paints in acrylic with a palette knife and spatula, mostly large portraits of women built up in thick layers of colour.

Global Art Monitor has documented 6 auction lots by Zara Muse, offered between 18 September 2024 and 21 October 2026 at Tate Ward, Bonhams and Christie's. The most recent on our record is Unspoken (2025), Christie's, 21 October 2026, sold for £30,720 with buyer's premium. Source: Christie's sold lots.

About the artist

Her work first appeared at auction in September 2024, when In Her Own Light sold at Tate Ward in London. Since then her paintings have been offered at Tate Ward, Bonhams and Christie's.

The table below lists every public sale of a painting that we have been able to document. Prints are tracked separately and will be added once we have checked each result.

Why the work is valued

Zara Muse's reputation so far rests on her auction record rather than on museum shows or prizes. We have not found her work in a public museum collection or a major museum exhibition in the sources we have read. Six of her paintings have sold at three London houses since September 2024, and each one with a confirmed estimate sold well above its high estimate. That shows strong bidding on those days. It is too short a record to show how her market will hold up over time.

Global Art Monitor does not sell art. A gallery elsewhere in the Artiscope group sells her work.
