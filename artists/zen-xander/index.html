Zen Xander

Zen Xander is a name with two published descriptions. Art Plugged introduced Zen Xander in 2024 as an anonymous street artist with a satirical approach, while the Zen Xander website describes a small group of painters, illustrators, designers and writers. The paintings put animals in human situations, such as a lion in a virtual reality headset eating salad.

About the artist

We have found no public auction result for Zen Xander as of 25 November 2026. We searched the lot archives of Phillips and Tate Ward and general auction listings. That does not mean no work has ever been sold at auction, only that none is documented in the sources we checked.

The first solo show, Society Falling, ran in Fitzrovia, London, from 26 June to 31 July 2024, according to Artsy. The gallery that held the show sells the work and is part of the Artiscope group, which publishes this site.

Why the work is valued

We found no museum show or collection in the sources we read. The gallery records Zen Xander works shown at South Place Hotel in London from September 2024 and at a Dorsett hotel in London in July 2024.
