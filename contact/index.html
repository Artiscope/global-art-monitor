Global Art Monitor does not sell art. It is part of Artiscope and reports public auctions and their results for the artists it covers. A gallery elsewhere in the Artiscope group sells work by some of those artists, including Zara Muse, Konn Artiss, Zen Xander, Los-Talgia, Frank Canvas, Robi Walters and Amanza Smith.
