Prices come from auction house lot pages and public auction databases, and are shown in the currency of the sale, marked by the symbol or code before each figure. "Our calculation" marks a figure the house did not publish. When only the total is published, we work back to the hammer price. When only the hammer price is reported, as with Sotheby's, which shows totals only to signed in users, we work forward to the total. Either way we use the buyer's premium rates the house published for that sale, and we check the result against what the house or the press reported. Point at the words in a row to see the working. How we report a price

Two prices, never one

Hammer and premium-inclusive prices are shown in separate columns and never merged.

Currency as sold

Figures stay in the currency of the sale. Any conversion carries its date and rate.

A source on every row

Each result links to the lot page or database it came from. Gaps are labelled, not filled.
