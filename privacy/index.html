Privacy

How we handle your information.

Last updated 26 November 2026

This page explains what happens to information about you when you use Global Art Monitor. The short version: there is no advertising and no analytics, the site sets no cookies, and the only personal details we keep are the ones you send us yourself.

Who is responsible

Global Art Monitor is published by Artiscope Ltd, which is responsible for personal information collected through this site. For anything on this page, write to info@artiscope.co.uk.

Artiscope Ltd is registered in England and Wales, company number 16048878.

If you use the contact form

The contact form sends us your name, your email address, any topic you pick, and your message, with the time it was sent and which of our sites it came from. We use them to read your message and reply. We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not pass your details to anyone else.

The law calls our reason for keeping them legitimate interests: you have written to us, and we need your message and address to answer you.

The messages are kept for us by Supabase, a database service, on servers in Ireland. Supabase also keeps short-lived technical records of each request so it can run and secure its service. Only the Artiscope team can read the messages.

We delete form messages 12 months after our last reply, sooner if you ask. The only exception is a message we need to keep to deal with a complaint or a legal claim, and then only for as long as that takes.

If you email us or ask to join a list

If you email us, we see your address and whatever you write, and use them to reply.

The subscribe boxes on our sites do not send anything themselves. They open your own email app with a message to us. If you send it, we keep your name and email address in our email inbox and use them only to send you what you asked for. That rests on your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time by writing to us. We keep your address until you leave the list.

What happens when a page loads

Whenever your browser fetches a web page, the server it asks sees some technical details, such as your IP address, your browser and the page you wanted. These services are involved here:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site, and GitHub keeps server records to run and protect its service.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser asks Google's servers for them.
  • unpkg supplies one small script that smooths the scrolling.
  • Supabase receives anything you send through the contact form, as described above.

We use them because a website needs them to work, which again counts as legitimate interests. GitHub and Google are based in the United States, so some of these details may be handled there. Each company's privacy policy explains how it looks after them.

Cookies and your browser

The site sets no cookies and contains no tracking or advertising code.

The site keeps a couple of small notes in your own browser for the length of your visit: that you have already seen the opening animation, or a pop-up, so they do not play again on every page. These notes stay on your device and are never sent to us. Closing the tab clears the short-lived ones, and you can clear the rest in your browser settings at any time.

Links to other sites

Our pages link to their sources. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and what happens there is up to them.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it or limit how we use it. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

You also have the right to object to us using your information where we rely on legitimate interests. If you object, we will stop unless we have a strong reason to carry on that outweighs yours.

To use any of these rights, write to info@artiscope.co.uk. We will answer within one month. We do not sell your information and we do not make automated decisions about you.

If you are unhappy

Please tell us first, at info@artiscope.co.uk. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, look into it and tell you what we found.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
