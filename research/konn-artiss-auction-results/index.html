Konn Artiss Auction Results: Early Secondary-Market Activity

By the Global Art Monitor research desk. Published 11 November 2026. 5 minute read.

Konn Artiss is better known for interventions than for objects. The name has been attached to a floating protest in Venice and to blocks of ice left outside Frieze London. Works have now started to appear at auction. Here is what has sold, and how much weight two results can carry.

Who is selling what

Konn Artiss works anonymously. The Art Newspaper has called it an "elusive art collective", while other reports describe a single street artist. For market purposes the distinction matters less than this: until 2026 there was no public resale record at all.

Untitled (2025) was lot 173 in Tate Ward's online sale with Artsy, By Collectors For Collectors, which closed on 14 May 2026. It carried an estimate of £6,000 to £8,000 and sold for £10,625 with premium. Tate Ward does not show hammer prices on its results pages, so we worked this one back from the total: about £8,500, a little above the high estimate.

Gilded Promise (2025) was lot 160 at Tate Ward with the same £6,000 to £8,000 estimate. It sold for £15,000 with premium, which implies a hammer price of about £12,000, or one and a half times the high estimate. We have not yet confirmed the sale date, so that cell is marked.

What two sales can tell you

  • There is a bid. Both lots sold, and both sold above the top of their estimates. Somebody was willing to pay those sums in public.
  • The works reached auction quickly. Both are dated 2025 and both were resold by 2026. Fast resale can mean demand. It can also mean an early owner chose to take a price. The results do not say which.
  • Everything so far is at one house. A record built at a single saleroom is narrower than one tested at several, with different audiences and different specialists setting the estimates.

What they cannot tell you

Two results are not a market. They do not show how many bidders took part, who consigned the works, or what the same pieces would make if offered again. They say nothing about works that may have been offered privately or withdrawn.

We will add each new sale to the Konn Artiss market profile as it is documented, including any lot that fails to sell. Unsold lots belong in the record too.
