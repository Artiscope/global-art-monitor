Zara Muse Auction Results: A Documented Market Timeline

By the Global Art Monitor research desk. Published 11 November 2026. 6 minute read.

Zara Muse has a short public auction record: four priced results in under two years, at two London houses, with two more lots at a third that we have not yet been able to price. This is what the record shows, and where it runs out.

The record so far

Each row below keeps three figures apart: the estimate the house published before the sale, the hammer price, and the total the buyer paid once the buyer's premium was added. All four priced sales were in pounds.

What the figures show

The first result came on 18 September 2024, when In Her Own Light (2023) sold at Tate Ward for a hammer price of £27,000 against an estimate of £6,000 to £8,000. With premium the buyer paid £33,750. Three months later The Shape of Stillness (2024) made a hammer price of £38,000 at the same house, against £8,000 to £12,000, for a total of £47,500.

The highest documented price is still the Bonhams sale of 3 April 2025. Malala Yousafzai, a portrait painted in 2019, sold for £51,200 with premium in the 20th/21st Century Art Day Sale at New Bond Street. Bonhams does not publish hammer prices, so we worked that one back from the total: about £40,000.

The most recent priced result is lower. Alya (2023) closed at £33,280 with premium in a Bonhams online sale on 18 June 2026, against an estimate of £7,000 to £10,000. Our calculated hammer price is £26,000.

How to read it

Three things stand out, and each needs a caution.

  • Every priced lot beat its estimate by a wide margin. Hammer prices came in at roughly three times the high estimate at Tate Ward and about two and a half times at Bonhams in 2026. That tells you bidders went well past the guide. It also tells you the guides were set low compared with where bidding ended.
  • The record has not risen in a straight line. The totals run £33,750, £47,500, £51,200 and then £33,280. These are four different paintings, of different sizes and subjects, sold in different kinds of sale. The dip is not proof of a fall, and the earlier climb was not proof of a trend.
  • The sample is small. Four priced results cannot tell you what a fifth painting will make. They show what four works made on four days.

What we could not confirm

Auction databases list two paintings at Christie's in London: Ava, offered on 2 July 2026 with an estimate starting at £6,000, and Unspoken (2025). We have not been able to check either price against a Christie's lot page, so both rows are marked as unconfirmed. We also have not confirmed the estimate Bonhams published for Malala Yousafzai.

A note on the 2025 record. Some reports give the Malala Yousafzai price in US dollars, at about $67,200. That is a conversion of the same £51,200 sale, not a separate result. We show every price in the currency of the sale.

If you hold a catalogue, invoice or saleroom notice that fills one of these gaps, tell us and we will update the timeline and credit the source.
